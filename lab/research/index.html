Research

Our work combines mathematical insights with rigorous experiments in optimization, end-to-end model efficiency, data, and technical alignment and safety.

Optimization

How do optimizer choice, batch size, and hyperparameters shape learning? We study stochastic training dynamics and use that understanding to design and analyze practical algorithms.

End-to-end model efficiency

Choices made at one stage affect the cost and performance of later stages. We study the full pipeline, from pre-training and supervised fine-tuning to reinforcement learning, distillation, and inference.

Our goal is to make compute, data, and memory go further while developing models that are well suited to the next stage.

Data
